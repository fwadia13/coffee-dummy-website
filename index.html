— About Us —

A Century of Coffee

The Copper Kettle was born in 1923 when Margaret & Theodore Holt opened a small tea house on Bunder Street. A kettle on the stove, mismatched chairs, and a love for good conversation.

Three generations later, we still roast our beans the same way — slow, patient, and by hand. The recipes haven't changed. The warmth hasn't changed. Only the city around us has grown.

We believe a great cup of coffee is an act of care. Come sit with us.
